### **2. Core Problem Definition & Importance**

*   **The Issue:** Catheter-Associated Urinary Tract Infections (CAUTIs) are one of the most common and preventable hospital-acquired infections. They lead to increased patient morbidity, longer hospital stays, and significant financial burden on the healthcare system. The primary driver of CAUTI risk is the prolonged, and often unnecessary, duration of catheterization.
*   **Why It's Important to Improve:** This project directly aligns with several key institutional and national standards, including:
    *   **National Patient Safety Goals:** Reducing the risk of healthcare-associated infections.
    *   **Nursing-Sensitive Indicators:** CAUTI rates are a direct measure of the quality of nursing care.
    *   **High Reliability & Patient Safety:** Proactively addressing a known source of patient harm is a core principle of a high-reliability organization.
    *   **Financial Impact:** Preventing CAUTIs reduces unreimbursed costs associated with hospital-acquired conditions.
